What happens after the 30-day trial?
After your 30-day free trial ends, you'll be automatically subscribed to the plan you selected. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends without being charged.
What's included in the Custom Plan?
The Custom Plan includes everything in the Pro Plan, plus unlimited monthly orders, a dedicated account manager, custom integrations with higher API rate limits, custom SLA with priority onboarding, and advanced security features including SAML SSO log-in. Pricing is based on your specific needs.
How many stores can I connect?
Both plans include unlimited connected stores. The Custom Plan offers additional benefits for large-scale operations with multiple locations and high-volume processing.
Do I need a credit card to start?
No credit card is required to start your 30-day free trial. You'll only need to add payment details when your trial is about to end.
